# Début Design System — Afterglow Line

## Direction

Début should feel like an editorial invitation to an evening out: warm, confident, atmospheric, and distinctly hospitality-led. The interface moves from champagne daylight into oxblood nightlife while keeping food, events, booking, and pickup easy to use.

## Palette

- Canvas: `#F7F0E5`
- Soft paper: `#EEDFCC`
- Ink: `#263027`
- Wine: `#7A3040`
- Deep wine: `#55242F`
- Clay: `#C76D49`
- Sage: `#DCE3D3`
- Brass: `#9A641F`
- On-dark: `#FFF8EF`

Normal text on clay uses `#181A17`. Brass is reserved for rules, focus, and larger accents; small labels on paper use wine.

## Typography and hierarchy

- Playfair Display carries editorial headlines and emotional moments.
- DM Sans carries navigation, controls, metadata, and body copy.
- Headlines may be expressive; controls must remain literal and readable.
- Avoid repetitive eyebrow labels when the following heading already supplies the context.

## Layout and media

- Use asymmetric editorial compositions with generous breathing room.
- Light surfaces should dominate; dark surfaces belong to media overlays and the closing footer.
- Preserve the supplied venue photo, vertical reel, food creative, and wide nightlife reel on the homepage.
- Menu food-item artwork remains functional content and should not be replaced by venue media.

## Interaction rules

- Use clear text actions such as “Reserve,” “Previous,” “Next,” and “Close.”
- Do not use arrow emoji or decorative Unicode glyphs as interface controls.
- Keep keyboard focus visible, controls at least 44px where practical, and reduced-motion behavior intact.
- Every responsive change should preserve hierarchy rather than merely shrinking the desktop layout.
