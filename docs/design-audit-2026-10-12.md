# Début visual audit — 12 October 2026

Reviewed the home, restaurant, events, booking, contact, and menu pages at 320, 390, 768, and 1440 px. The site has no horizontal overflow at those widths.

## Changes made

- Replaced repeated italic headline styling with upright type. Kept gold emphasis on the main page introductions; secondary headlines rely on scale and spacing.
- Reduced repetitive uppercase section labels and removed decorative stars without a purpose.
- Rewrote generic nightlife language with details tied to Début: its downtown Barrie address, dining room, bar, music, menu, and October events.
- Replaced the menu's repeated slogan strip with the venue name, address, and menu context.
- Added the exact official logo and October event poster supplied by the owner.
- Replaced “3D image” labels on static dish artwork with “illustration” and “view dish.” The image viewer does not claim rotation or AR.
- Checked top and lower sections visually on desktop and mobile, including the poster layout and booking action.

## Remaining asset constraint

The food imagery is illustrative rather than photography or rotatable 3D. Venue photography and the event poster are authentic assets. Future real dish photography or approved 3D scans would improve the menu's editorial credibility and enable the requested 360°/AR experience.
